About us

A family orchard, grown on sun & mist.

Sunmist Fruit is a thriving plantation in Temecula, California — over 100 acres across four farm locations, and one of the bigger fresh-fruit farms in the valley.

Tucked into a private valley in the De Luz hills, Sunmist Fruit is a working farm. Warm days and cool coastal mist give our citrus its signature sweetness — the same fruit we ship across California is waiting on the trees for you.

The unique micro-climate of the Temecula Valley brings ample sun and a bountiful, nearly year-round harvest. We are home to more than 20,000 Meyer lemon trees producing almost five million pounds a year, plus over a million pounds of sweet, juicy Tango tangerines.

Our aim is simple: grow and ship the tastiest, highest-quality fruit — and share the farm with you. Sunmist is a place for U-Pick, fruit wholesale, weddings and all kinds of events.
