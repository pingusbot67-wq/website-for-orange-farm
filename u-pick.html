01U-Pick

How U-Pick works

Three easy steps between you and a bag full of sunshine. Open every Saturday and Sunday of the season.

  1. 1

    Reserve

    Buy your entry tickets below, or RSVP by email, phone or WeChat so we can plan the day.

  2. 2

    Pick

    Grab a bag and a picking pole, then roam: tangerines everywhere, lemons by the parking lot, blueberries on the far side, avocados up top.

  3. 3

    Weigh & pay

    Bring your haul to the scale and pay by the pound. We accept cash, Zelle and Venmo.
