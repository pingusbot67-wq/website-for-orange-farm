01Our grove

A family orchard, grown on sun & mist.

Tucked into a private valley in the De Luz hills, Sunmist Fruit is a working farm. Warm days and cool coastal mist give our citrus its signature sweetness — the same fruit we ship across California is waiting on the trees for you.

Bring the kids, bring a picnic, and spend a slow morning between the rows. We hand you a bag and a picking pole; the orchard does the rest.

Tango Tangerines

Seedless. Easy to peel. Sweet as candy.

Our signature crop — over a million pounds a year. Tango is a seedless mandarin bred in California: bright, juicy and so easy to peel that kids finish a bag before the car leaves the lot.

Meyer Lemons

The chef's lemon — floral, sweet, thin-skinned.

03U-Pick

How U-Pick works

Three easy steps between you and a bag full of sunshine. Open every Saturday and Sunday of the season.

  1. 1

    Reserve

    Book your weekend visit with the form below, by phone, or on WeChat so we can plan the day.

  2. 2

    Pick

    Grab a bag and a picking pole, then roam: tangerines everywhere, lemons by the parking lot, blueberries on the far side, avocados up top.

  3. 3

    Weigh & pay

    Bring your haul to the scale and pay by the pound. We accept cash, Zelle and Venmo.
