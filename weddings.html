05Questions

Good to know.

How many guests can you host?

Up to 1,000 guests for a ceremony, across up to 50,000 sq ft of space.

How much does a wedding cost?

Weddings start from $4,000 and other special events from $2,000. Tell us your date and guest count for an exact quote.

What's included?

Tables and chairs, a dressing room with kitchen and restroom, projector and screen, speaker, outside bar, BBQ grill and a wedding arch.

Can our guests stay overnight?

Yes — our vacation home sleeps up to 20, and the private orchard villa sleeps 8.

Where is the venue?

26700 Avenida Del Oro, Temecula, CA 92590 — about 1.5 hours from Los Angeles and 10 minutes from Old Town Temecula.

Can we visit first?

Of course. Send the form below or call 626-898-7800 and we'll set up a tour of the grounds.
